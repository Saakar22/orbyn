## Live demo in 90 seconds

1. **Landing (0:00).** Open https://orbyn-mu.vercel.app and scroll once through the story.
2. **Sign up (0:10).** Get started, fill in the form; the strength meter reacts as you type.
3. **Onboarding (0:25).** Business name, "Start with sample data", PayPal sandbox step. Open the dashboard.
4. **Ask (0:40).** Assistant: "Create an invoice for Alex for $1,000 for website development." The approval card appears.
5. **Approve (0:50).** Approve & issue, then Open PayPal checkout.
6. **Pay (0:55).** Pay part, $300, Debit or Credit Card. Card `4012 0000 3333 0026`, any future expiry, any CVV, a US billing address. Choose **Pay now as guest**.
7. **Verified (1:15).** The pay page shows Partially paid. Transactions → Verified by PayPal lists the capture id.
8. **Sign out (1:25).** Account menu → Sign out; the landing page confirms it.

Prefer no sign-up? "Try the live demo" opens a private guest workspace with labelled sample data.
